## About

Prof. Dr. S. Balaji Science Academy, based in Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu, coaches students for the CBSE and Tamil Nadu State Board examinations: CBSE Class 10 Science, CBSE Class 12 Physics and Chemistry, and State Board Class 12 Physics and Chemistry.

This site brings the academy's teaching together in one place: course information, free learning tools, and a login-based platform where enrolled students practise questions and sit timed mock tests.

## Courses

- CBSE Class 10 Science
- CBSE Class 12 Physics and Chemistry
- Tamil Nadu State Board Class 12 Physics and Chemistry

Each course has its own page describing the syllabus coverage, teaching approach and how to join.

## Practice and mock test platform

Enrolled students sign in with their Google account and are approved by the academy. They then see the subjects, practice questions and mock tests set for their class.

**For students**
- **Practice by chapter.** Choose a chapter, difficulty and question type, work through the questions, see the results, and download or share them as a PDF.
- **Timed mock tests.** Full papers with a countdown timer, a question navigator and mark-for-review. Tests can be resumed if interrupted, and the clock keeps running.
- **Answers released only after submission.** Correct answers are not sent to the device during a test.
- **Negative marking and shuffling** where the paper is set up for them.
- **Results as PDF.** After a mock test, students can download or share a PDF of their result.
- **Science that reads properly.** Equations, derivatives, vectors and chemical formulae are typeset on every screen, including phones.
- **Eleven question types.** Single and multiple correct choice, true or false, fill in the blank, numerical, assertion and reason, match the following, case-based, and very short, short and long answers.

**For the academy**
- A management console for approving students and managing their access.
- A question bank organised by board, class, subject and chapter, with images, difficulty levels and marks.
- Bulk upload of questions from Excel, with a check report before anything is saved.
- A paper builder for fixed papers and automatically generated papers, with timing, negative marking and visibility controls.
- Question-level analytics to judge how hard each question really is, with no student identity shown.
- A catalogue that lets the academy add new boards, classes and subjects as its programmes grow.

## Design

Clean, minimal and fully responsive, designed to work as well on a phone as on a desktop. Typography and colour follow a quiet, institutional style, and the pages are written with accessibility in mind.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages, with Firebase for sign-in and data, ImageKit for question images, a Cloudflare Worker for secure image uploads, and MathJax for mathematics and chemistry.
